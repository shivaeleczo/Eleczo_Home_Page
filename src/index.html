Modules that cannot be built yet

Shown blocked, not mocked up. Designing UI for data that may never arrive produces a convincing prototype backed by nothing.

  • BLOCKED · DS-11

    Shop by application

    Needs a curated application → product-type mapping dataset, authored by someone who understands electrical specification. It is domain data, not UI.

    Needs: named domain-expert owner + delivery date

  • BLOCKED · DS-11

    Shop by industry

    Same dependency as application shopping. Assumption AS-006 holds that no such dataset currently exists.

    Needs: the same mapping dataset

  • BLOCKED · CLM-10

    Shop by brand

    Selling a manufacturer's products does not by itself confer the right to display its marks. Authorisation is a contract question per brand.

    Needs: per-brand logo authorisation (D-4)

  • BLOCKED · M-20

    Buying guides

    Selection guidance — breaking capacity, curve choice, cable sizing — either exists as editorial content or must be authored. Unknown which.

    Needs: content audit, then an author

  • BLOCKED · DS-07

    Product cards with price

    Price display determines cacheability before it determines layout. Whether trade pricing is public, gated or quote-only is undecided.

    Needs: pricing policy + counsel on MRP

  • BLOCKED · DS-16

    Seller identity on listings

    Whether sellers are buyer-visible or an invisible fulfilment mechanism changes the product card's data contract and its caching behaviour.

    Needs: commercial ruling
